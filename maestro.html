Maestro

A governed AI operations platform designed to place explicit identity, authority, commercial rights, capacity controls and audit around agentic and AI-assisted workflows.

IdentityAuthorityEntitlementsQuotaGovernanceExecutionIntegrationAudit

The problem

Organizations can prototype AI agents quickly, but operational adoption creates harder questions: who is allowed to execute what, under which subscription and quota, against which systems, with what evidence, and how should the runtime behave when a provider or authorization source fails?

The approach

Maestro acts as a control plane around execution. It separates the decision to allow an operation from the operation itself, and links that decision to identity, entitlements, quota, governance, capacity and audit. The design is intended to support multiple providers and enterprise integration patterns instead of locking the system to a single model vendor.

Commercial control

Plans, subscriptions, entitlements and quota influence runtime permission rather than living only in billing screens.

Fail-closed design

Sensitive operations are designed to stop when required authority or evidence is missing instead of silently assuming success.

Enterprise integration

Adapter-oriented integration supports controlled read, write and restricted scopes across external systems.

Auditability

Evidence and audit trails are treated as first-class operational outputs.

Current maturity

Maestro is beyond the concept/MVP stage and has undergone substantial technical qualification. A portable local evaluation runtime has been built around Docker, PostgreSQL and Redis, with migrations and smoke checks. Controlled external sandbox evidence also exists for enterprise/API interoperability.

Readiness note: evaluation-ready does not mean proven production deployment. Real staging and a measured customer POC/pilot remain separate gates before production-grade claims should be made.
