NARSY

An advanced multi-surface software system combining operational workspaces, tool and data-provider catalogs, subscription administration, runtime services and CGT-based evaluation components.

What it is

NARSY is being developed as a broad operational software environment rather than a single-function application. Current source inventories show workspace and web-app services, tool catalogs, operational tooling, subscription-administration surfaces, data-provider catalogs and multiple CGT evaluation modules.

Observed software areas

Operational context services · Workspace APIs · Tool catalogs · Subscription administration · Data-provider services · Frontend runtime/bootstrap · CGT evaluation and world/project analysis modules.

Current maturity

The project is reported as more than 80% complete and has multiple advanced working branches and test-oriented revisions. It is therefore presented here as an advanced pre-release system, not as a finished commercial product.

Why it matters

NARSY demonstrates a second line of work beyond Maestro: a large application surface where governed analysis, operational tools and subscription-aware services are brought together into a structured user environment.
