SIMQAN

An engineering-oriented software platform that brings together quantum-computing planning, provider-runtime support, chip-engineering resources and operational administration.

Observed project scope

Current source inventories show a broad software surface with quantum algorithm and gate catalogs, near-term execution planning, noise-calibration fixtures, provider runtime bootstrap, engineering resource hubs and multiple chip-engineering planning components.

Quantum planning

Algorithm catalogs, gate catalogs, near-term execution plans and quantum operational transition components.

Runtime support

Provider bootstrap and provider-facing runtime components designed to prepare execution against external quantum resources.

Engineering support

Chip-engineering hotspot, IP-core, NoC and reference-design planning assets, plus engineering-drawing resources.

Platform administration

Frontend administration, subscription surfaces and resource-hub catalogs indicate a product layer beyond the core planning logic.

Current maturity

SIMQAN is under active development with a wide test inventory spanning quantum planning, provider runtime, resource catalogs, engineering planning and administrative/bootstrap behavior. It is presented as an advanced research-and-engineering software project, not as a certified quantum-computing product.

Validation note: software support for quantum and chip-engineering workflows does not by itself establish hardware performance, physical correctness or independent engineering certification. Those claims require separate domain validation.
